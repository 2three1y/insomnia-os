Keyboard

  • Arrow keys move between desktop icons, Enter opens.
  • Escape closes the window you're in and returns you to its icon.
  • The grip button ⠿ on a title bar moves the window with arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps).
  • The taskbar lists open windows; the speaker button mutes everything.

Respects reduced motion, works with screen readers, and never makes a sound before you ask.
